HISTORICAL NOTES // TIKI MIXOLOGY PRINCIPLES

The Sacred Art of Rum Blending

Don the Beachcomber and Trader Vic rarely poured just one rum into a glass. True vintage tiki libations achieve their hypnotic depth by colliding disparate rum families:

  • High-Ester Jamaican Pot Still: Funk, ripe plantain, spice, and raw sugarcane punch.
  • French Rhum Agricole (Martinique): Fresh-crushed cane juice, grassy vegetal terroir, and bone-dry clarity.
  • Demerara (Guyana Wooden Stills): Smoked molasses, burnt sugar, leather, and deep naval heft.
